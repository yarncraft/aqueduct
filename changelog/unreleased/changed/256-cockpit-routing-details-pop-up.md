- **Cockpit: routing details open in a pop-up** (#256). Clicking a table, route or topic in the Routing graph opens its
  details centred over the page, like the event inspector, instead of in a panel below the graph. Close, Esc or a click
  outside closes it; an inspector opened from it shows on top, and Esc closes the inspector first. A route's details
  start with its rule (SELECT / WHERE / FROM / INTO / PARTITIONED-BY), in full, as on its card.
